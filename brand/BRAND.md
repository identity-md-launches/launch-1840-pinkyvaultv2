# Pinky — brand notes

## Name and line

- Name: **Pinky**. Token: **PINKY**.
- Line: **A promise with money behind it.**

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Wine | `#5E1224` | Avatar and header background, headings on light surfaces, "broken" status |
| Ivory | `#F4EEDD` | Text on wine, page and card background |
| Dusty pink | `#E79AA8` | The one accent: second hook of the mark, the line, "kept" status |

Dusty pink fades on ivory. On light surfaces draw the mark in wine and keep pink for fills, not
thin strokes or text.

## Mark

Two hooked pinkies: one ivory, one pink, on wine. Files: `avatar.svg` (400×400, safe inside X's
round crop) and `banner.svg` (1500×500, X header). PNGs sit next to them.

## Type

- **Fraunces**, weight 600, with the soft and wonky axes on (`'SOFT' 100, 'WONK' 1`): the wordmark, headings,
  numbers and the sentence on each promise card.
- **Nunito**: everything else.

Both are open fonts served by Google Fonts. Rounded, a little playful, still readable in a table.

## Voice

A friend who makes sure nobody gets played. Short, human, a little dry. Numbers stay, hype goes.

| Moment | Say |
|---|---|
| New promise | Someone just put 20 IMD on not selling for an hour. We'll see. |
| Kept | Kept their word. 20 IMD goes home. |
| Broken | They sold. The pinky's broken, and so is the 20 IMD. |
| Bio | Say you won't sell, and put IMD on it. We hold it until you've kept your word. |
| Empty list | No promises yet. Be the first to mean it. |

Avoid: "revolutionary", "to the moon", exclamation marks, lecturing, and the word "jeet".
